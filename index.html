Materials science

Materials laboratory

Material property

Yield strength

Stress at the onset of plastic deformation, under a chosen yield criterion. Highly dependent on alloy, heat treatment, and processing history.

Material property

Hardness

Resistance to local indentation. Hardness correlations with strength require material-specific calibration and uncertainty.

Material property

Toughness

Resistance to crack initiation and propagation; not interchangeable with hardness or yield strength.

Evidence boundaries

Museum-documented materials: may establish descriptive composition categories such as “steel” without quantifying elemental fractions or properties.

Modern engineering parameters: are hypothetical user inputs to the simulator, not measurements of historical blades.

Machine learning: no model or dataset has been trained, validated or connected in this version.

Interactive material physics · hypothetical modern parameters

Elastic response and onset of yield

Change elastic modulus and yield stress to calculate the approximate strain at first yield in a uniaxial linear-elastic model. This is educational modelling; the values are not measurements of any museum sword.

—

Assumes a constant Young's modulus, linear elasticity up to first yield and homogeneous isotropic material. No plastic constitutive model, hardness conversion, metallography, heat-treatment simulation or fracture prediction.
